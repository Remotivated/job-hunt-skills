PASS if the reply briefly explains what Job Hunt Skills does and then asks the user how they want to start, for example by pasting a resume and a job posting, building a resume from scratch, or confirming the folder where their files will be saved. The reply may also say that nothing is saved until they ask, but that does not replace the explanation and the question.

FAIL if it says it has already created a workspace, folders or files, or if it opens with a list of intake questions before saying what the plugin does.
