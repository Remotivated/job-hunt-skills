## How to use them

1. Open a prompt page and copy the box.
2. Paste it into a **fresh chat** and add your material where marked — usually one or two pastes, sometimes just a line.
3. Read the first response, then use the follow-up moves listed on each page. The prompts are conversations, not forms: the first paste gets you the headline result, the follow-ups get you depth.

## Why these prompts are different

Most AI job-search output fails the same way: it looks impressive right up until an interviewer asks a follow-up question. These prompts are built backwards from that moment. Every one of them forbids the model from inventing metrics, upgrading "contributed to" into "led," expanding "AWS" into services you never named, or quietly dropping your honest qualifiers. When a stronger output would need a fact you didn't provide, you get a question instead of a fabrication.

That's the deal these prompts make: **everything they produce, you can defend out loud with a straight face.**

The last word is still yours. When a prompt hands you a pre-send checklist or flags a claim to verify, treat it as a blocker, not a suggestion — confirm it or cut it. And when your source material is thin, let the model ask its questions before you push for a draft; a slower first answer beats polished fiction.
